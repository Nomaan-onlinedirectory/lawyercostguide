What Drives the Cost in Corpus Christi

Fee structure depends on claim typeEmployment law in Corpus Christi uses different fee arrangements for different matters. Discrimination and wrongful termination claims are typically taken on contingency — no upfront fee. Contract review, severance negotiation, and non-compete analysis are typically hourly ($250–$450/hr) or flat fee ($500–$1,500 for severance review).
EEOC filing deadlines are strictFederal discrimination claims in Corpus Christi must be filed with the EEOC within 180–300 days of the adverse action — depending on TX's laws. Missing this deadline permanently bars your federal claim. If you were recently terminated or disciplined for a potentially unlawful reason, act quickly regardless of whether you intend to sue.
Severance agreements waive rightsIf your Corpus Christi employer offers severance, the agreement almost certainly waives your right to sue for discrimination or wrongful termination. Never sign a severance agreement without an employment attorney reviewing it — the cost ($500–$1,500) is almost always justified given what you're waiving.
At-will employment limits but doesn't eliminate claimsTX is an at-will employment state — employers can dismiss for any reason or no reason. But this doesn't mean for any illegal reason. Dismissals motivated by race, sex, age (40+), disability, national origin, pregnancy, or retaliation for protected activity are still illegal regardless of at-will status.
State law adds protections beyond federalTX employment law may provide additional protections beyond federal law. Pregnancy leave, domestic violence leave, salary history bans, and non-compete enforceability all vary by state. An employment attorney familiar with TX-specific law can identify claims that exist under state law but not federal.
Money-saving tip for Corpus ChristiBefore retaining an employment attorney, document everything in writing — save emails, performance reviews, and any communications relevant to your claim. A well-documented case requires fewer billable hours for your attorney to build. For contingency cases, documentation quality directly affects settlement value. Always verify contractor licences with your state licensing board.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an employment lawyer cost in Corpus Christi?
Employment lawyer costs in Corpus Christi depend on the type of matter. Discrimination and wrongful termination claims are typically taken on contingency — no upfront fee, with the attorney taking 30–40% of any recovery. Hourly billing ($250–$450/hr) applies for contract review, severance negotiation, and advisory matters. Severance review is often flat fee ($500–$1,500).
Was my termination wrongful under TX law?
TX is an at-will employment state, meaning employers can generally dismiss without reason. However, dismissal for race, sex, age (40+), disability, national origin, pregnancy, religion, or in retaliation for protected activity (whistleblowing, FMLA leave, workers' comp claims) is illegal. State law may add protections beyond federal law. A Corpus Christi employment attorney can evaluate your specific situation.
How long do I have to file an employment claim in Corpus Christi?
Federal discrimination claims must be filed with the EEOC within 180–300 days of the adverse action in TX. TX state law claims may have different deadlines. Missing these deadlines permanently bars your claim. If you were recently dismissed or disciplined for potentially unlawful reasons, consult an employment attorney in Corpus Christi within 30 days.
Should I sign my severance agreement in Corpus Christi?
Do not sign a severance agreement without having an employment attorney review it. Severance agreements in Corpus Christi typically waive all claims against your employer — including discrimination claims worth potentially much more than the severance offered. Attorney review costs $500–$1,500 and almost always justifies itself. You typically have 21 days to consider an offer.
What is the EEOC process in Corpus Christi?
Before filing a federal discrimination lawsuit from Corpus Christi, you must file a charge with the EEOC. The EEOC may investigate, offer mediation, or issue a 'right to sue' letter. The process typically takes 6–18 months. Many Corpus Christi employment attorneys handle the EEOC charge phase on contingency or flat fee before committing to full litigation.
How do I verify an employment lawyer's licence in TX?
Verify any TX attorney's bar registration at State Bar of Texas: texasbar.com. For employment matters, also check whether the attorney is a member of the National Employment Law Council (NELC) or National Employment Lawyers Association (NELA) — membership indicates specialist employment law practice.
